What we check

The AI-era risks generic web shops never look at, plus the security basics most sites still fail.

01

Your AI surface

Chatbots, AI agents, and automation widgets embedded in your site, exposed API keys sitting in your page's code, and open AI or agent endpoints anyone can reach. This is the new attack surface, and it's the part nobody else audits.

02

Where your data can leak

We map where customer and client data flows into AI tools and whether it's actually protected on the way. Prompt injection, over-shared context, and third-party AI services are all paths for sensitive data to walk out the door.

03

Security headers and TLS

The baseline hardening most sites get wrong: HSTS, content-security-policy, clickjacking protection, and a healthy certificate. We grade your posture and show you exactly what's missing.

04

Exposed and outdated software

Public version leaks, exposed files like .git and .env, directory listings, and out-of-date platforms, the exact things attackers scan the whole internet for, every day.

05

Email spoofing protection

SPF, DKIM, and DMARC decide whether a scammer can send email that looks like it's from you. We check all three, because for a business that emails clients, this is reputation and trust on the line.

06

Privacy and compliance signals

If you handle health, financial, or personal data, regulators care how you protect it. We check the signals that matter for PIPEDA in Canada and the NDPA in Nigeria, so you know where you stand before someone else asks.

Questions

What exactly is an "AI security review"?
It's a security check built for the AI era. On top of the usual web security basics (headers, TLS, exposed files, email spoofing), we specifically look at the AI you've added: chatbots, agents, automations, and the ways they can leak data or be manipulated. Most reviews skip that entirely. Ours leads with it.
We only added a simple chatbot. Do we really need this?
A "simple" chatbot often has access to more than its owner realizes: your content, sometimes your customer messages, sometimes keys embedded right in the page. That's exactly the kind of thing that leaks quietly. The free review takes a few minutes of your time and tells you whether you're fine or not. No downside to knowing.
Is the review safe? Could it break our site?
Yes, it's safe. The review is passive and non-intrusive: we look at your site from the outside, the way a visitor or an attacker would, using only public information. We don't log in, we don't fuzz, and we don't do anything that could take your site down. No access to your systems is ever required.
What do you do with our data?
For the free review, we only use your public website URL. We don't ask for credentials, and we don't store your business data. If you go deeper with a paid audit, we handle everything under a clear scope and delete working data when the engagement ends. Privacy is the product, we'd be poor at our job otherwise.
We're a care agency and handle sensitive client data. Does this cover PIPEDA?
Yes. Regulated data (health, personal, financial) is exactly where this matters most, and it's a sweet spot for us. The review flags where sensitive intake could be exposed and reads your posture against PIPEDA in Canada and the NDPA in Nigeria, so you know where you stand before a regulator, an auditor, or a client asks.
