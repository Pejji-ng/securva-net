The 6 factors

Score yes/no on each. The full PDF gives you the why, the fix, and the verification step for each.

01

Legacy tier nobody is patching

Old internal server (Windows IIS, ASP.NET, PHP admin panel) running on the same network as the modern customer site. Patches do not land here.

→ Is there a server you have not patched in 6 months?
02

Missing HSTS on customer-facing hosts

One web-server config line. Industry standard since 2012. We scanned 1,180 Nigerian sites: 64% are missing it.

→ Does your site send Strict-Transport-Security headers?
03

Forgotten and shadow infrastructure

Subdomains from old campaigns. Staging sites never taken down. Test environments nobody owns. The attacker enumerates these even when you do not.

→ Can you list every subdomain your org controls from memory?
04

Concentrated ASN with many edge hosts

Banks own hundreds of public hosts on their own networks. Each one is a potential entry point. You only need to defend ALL of them.

→ Do you have 10+ public hosts with no documented owner?
05

Credentials in code repositories

Every Nigerian fintech we have disclosed to in the last 30 days had this: a developer committed an .env file to a public GitHub repo. Live credentials inside.

→ Has anyone on your team EVER committed an .env file?
06

No culture of breach notification

NDPA 2023 mandates notification within 72 hours of awareness. Most Nigerian breaches in 2025-2026 stayed silent or were leaked by media first.

→ If a breach happened today, do you have a 72-hour process?
