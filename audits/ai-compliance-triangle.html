The problem

If you ship AI features in Nigeria, you are subject to:

  • NDPA 2023 + NDPC enforcement. ₦7.2 billion in fines collected to date. 1,368 financial institutions served compliance notices. Section 47 exposure ranges from ₦10M minimum up to 2% of annual gross revenue.

Three regulators. One AI deployment. Most teams audit one regulator at a time, miss the harmonization, and pay three times.
